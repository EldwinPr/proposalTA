## ISO/IEC 17025 — IMPORTANT NUANCE

- SSLab (the testing unit) was founded in **2025** — it is the newest line, NOT long-established.
- Accreditation under ISO/IEC 17025 is **per-parameter / per-method (ruang lingkup)**, not lab-wide.
- **Some parameters ARE accredited; not the full testing scope.** "It's more complex than it seems" (user).
- KAN accreditation is also held by the sister unit **Sentral Sistem Calibration (2014)** for calibration scope.
- Correct proposal framing: "SSLab memegang akreditasi ISO/IEC 17025 untuk sebagian parameter pengujiannya"
  — NOT "sudah terakreditasi penuh" / "already accredited" (full lab).
